Up and running before your next standup

LingoMic is a Chrome extension, with a Windows app for desktop meeting apps. No need to switch the meeting tools your company already uses.

  1. Step 1

    Add to Chrome

    Install from the Chrome Web Store, sign in with Google and paste a free Gemini key. Your first 30 minutes of full access start right away, no card needed.

  2. Step 2

    Join a call and just talk

    Turn on “Translate my voice” in the LingoMic panel and speak naturally. Everyone else hears you in their language while you get captions, notes and answers.

  3. Step 3

    Unlock a 5-day free trial

    When your free 30 minutes are up, start a 5-day trial with full access. Your card is charged only if you stay past day 5.
