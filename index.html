LingoMic

Live transcription, translation and AI notes for Google Meet, Zoom (web) and Microsoft Teams (web). It works in your browser; no bot joins your call.

Features

  • Live captions with the speaker's name when the meeting page shows it
  • Sentence-by-sentence translation into 24 languages
  • LingoMic notes: summary, decisions, action items, open questions
  • Ask AI about the meeting, during or after it
  • Optional voice translation through a virtual microphone
  • Searchable dashboard, export to Markdown, TXT or JSON
  • 10 interface languages, light and dark mode

Bring your own key

You use your own Google Gemini API key (a free one from Google AI Studio is enough to start). Your meetings and notes stay in your browser. Read the privacy policy.
