Log in to LingoMic

Your account lives in the LingoMic extension, so you sign in there with Google. A web dashboard for your license and devices is coming later.

  1. 1Add LingoMic to Chrome, or open it if it is already installed.
  2. 2Click the LingoMic icon in the toolbar and choose Sign in with Google.
  3. 3Join a Meet, Zoom or Teams call in the browser and start speaking.
