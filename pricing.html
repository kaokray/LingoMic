Billing questions

Will my card be charged after the trial?

Yes, on day 5, for the plan shown at checkout (6 months, $29, unless you picked another). Cancel before then and you pay nothing. We email you two days ahead.

Why is it cheaper than other tools?

Paid plans run on your own free Gemini key, so we don't pay for AI on your behalf and don't need a monthly fee.

How long does launch pricing last?

Launch prices hold for the first 300 customers. After that, plans move to their regular price.

Does my plan renew automatically?

The 6-month and 1-year plans renew at the same price until you cancel, and we email you a week before each renewal. Lifetime never renews. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. After that, new recording and AI features pause, and past meetings stay open and exportable.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes. Every purchase comes with a receipt, and you can add your company name and tax ID at checkout.

Which devices can I use?

Your plan is tied to the Google account you sign in with, for one person. Use it in Chrome and in the Windows app.
